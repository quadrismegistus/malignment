# superego_stages: which stage installs the diegetic superego?

Commissioned by the dario seat; design narrowed and widened by RH, 2026-09-24. **This question was chosen after seeing two results**: Y (`passage_analysis/diegetic_superego`: aligned endpoints attach guilt, consent hesitation and moral comment inside sexual scenes they keep writing; SUPEREGO_IN_SCENE given sexual_scene 15.18 -> 21.60, median pair +4.30pp, 24/32) and `division_of_labour/sft_share` (the SFT checkpoint sits a median 0.82 of the way at the word grain). Y has two checkpoints per lineage; this adds the stages between them.

## Generation: Y's settings, checked, on Y's engine

Y (`malign-logits scripts/vllm_y_run.py`, `data/y_run_manifest.json`): vLLM, raw, the bare stem plus `" " + word`, temperature 1.0, top_p 1.0, top_k unset (disabled in vLLM), 256 new tokens, float16, 50 samples per cell, 34 cells (5 stems; `prompts/y_cells.jsonl`, built from `data/y_shard_00.json`). The new rungs use `malignment.vllm_generate` with exactly those settings: `--n 50 --temperature 1.0 --top-p 1.0 --max-new-tokens 256 --dtype float16`, raw frame, the fixed render path (`render="ids_v2"`). **Checked 2026-09-24:** on OLMo-2-0425-1B the 14 prompt token ids Y stored are identical to today's render. The vLLM VERSION differs (Y's is unrecorded; now 0.22.1) and the hardware differs; both change which draws come out, not the distribution. Generated on RunPod (vLLM), never on this Mac (HF would be a second engine against Y's).

## Coding

Y's coder unchanged: `code_y_superego_v3` (`SuperegoV3Task`, deepseek-v4-flash, t=0; `malign-logits malign_logits/tasks/code_y_superego_v3.py`), its own `prepare()`, blinded. **Pass A as Y**: passages that ran the full 256 tokens, a seeded random draw of 20 per cell per model (seed 20260808, Y's manifest seed).

**Coder-drift gate, BEFORE any new passage is coded.** Y was coded in August; `deepseek-v4-flash` now resolves server-side to `deepseek-flash`. Re-code 40 randomly drawn Y pass-A passages (seed 20260924) and compare with their stored codes: if SUPEREGO_IN_SCENE and sexual_scene each agree on >= 36 of 40 (90%), Y's stored codes are re-used; otherwise every re-used Y passage on these ten ladders is re-coded with the new ones (cost roughly doubles), and the result says which happened.

## Outcome, steps, units

Primary measure, Y's: **SUPEREGO_IN_SCENE conditional on sexual_scene**, per model (share of pass-A passages coded sexual_scene YES that carry a superego tag). Per ladder:

    step1 = first rung - base              (SFT, or chat SFT for Amber)
    step2 = second rung - first rung        (DPO / preference / safety)
    step3 = RLVR - DPO                      (OLMo ladders only)
    total = last - base;   SFT share = step1 / total (where total > 0)

Unit = the ladder (10). Medians reported beside means (AmberSafe, +39.7 total in Y, dominates any ratio of means).

## Readings, fixed now

- **SFT step:** Wilcoxon signed-rank over ladders, step1 > 0, p < 0.05; sign test beside it (9/10 needed for p < 0.05).
- **Second step:** the same on step2. Expected underpowered if it is small (dario's simulation: ~0.45 power at 18 lineages when preference adds 20%), so the SFT SHARE with a bootstrap interval over ladders is the reported quantity, not a p-value on step2.
- **Verdict:** "SFT installs it" if step1 is significant and the median SFT share is >= 0.5; "the later stage installs it" if step2 is significant and the median share is < 0.5; otherwise reported as the interval.
- **Per-ladder readings**, declared only where Y's total is large enough to read against the per-model SE (~3.3pp at Y depth; ~5.7pp on step2 - step1): AmberSafe, OLMo-2-1B, CT-LLM, pythia-6.9b-hh. The other ladders are pooled, not read individually. Amber's split is chat SFT against safety SFT, and is reported as that.
- **RLVR (step3):** descriptive over three OLMo ladders; no test.

**The registered readings.** SFT step: 7 of 10 ladders positive, median +4.0, Wilcoxon p = 0.13, sign p = 0.34. Second step: 7 of 10, median +0.9, Wilcoxon p = 0.19, sign p = 0.34. SFT share over the 8 ladders with total > 0: median **0.69, bootstrap 95% [0.26, 1.08]**. **Verdict by the declared rule: neither "SFT installs it" nor "the later stage installs it"; reported as the interval.**

**The declared per-ladder readings split, and do not average.** SFT carries all of it on OLMo-2-1B (+17.1 of +17.3, step2 +0.3) and CT-LLM (+16.0, step2 -1.1). The later stage carries most of it on Amber (safety SFT +29.3 of +39.7, SE 3.6) and pythia-6.9b (HH DPO +8.7 of +14.0, SE 3.1). *Post hoc, not tested:* the two ladders where the later stage dominates are the two whose later stage is explicitly safety or harmlessness data, which would make the stage label (SFT vs preference) the wrong grain and the data the right one. It is a reading of two ladders.

**Also not predicted.** Alpaca SFT LOWERS it on llama-7b (-9.9), and beaver's safe-RLHF adds nothing (+0.5): the one ladder whose preference stage is safety-branded shows no in-scene superego from it. RLVR is positive on all three OLMo ladders (+2.7, +3.0, +7.1; only Olmo-3's exceeds 2 SE), descriptive as declared. The ratio is unstable where the total is small (pythia-2.8b 1.77, neo -0.95), which is why medians were declared.

- Olmo-3-7B's total was NEGATIVE in Y (-6.3pp), so a sign test cannot reach 10/10.
