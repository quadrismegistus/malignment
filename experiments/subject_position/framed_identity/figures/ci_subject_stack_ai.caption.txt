Each bar is one condition's answers split by the kind of thing the speaker claims to be, and the AI
answers further by the name they give. Value: per model, the share of its answers in that group; the
bar prints the MEAN over models (each model once; each bar sums to 100, asserted). Labels inside
segments are rounded to whole percents and printed only where the segment is at least 5 points wide;
exact values are below. The three AI slices sum to the AI segment of ci_subject_stack4 (asserted).

AI, names itself: the speaker's self_name or maker_named matches its OWN lineage. AI, names another
model: it names a known model or lab that is not its own (another wins where both occur). AI, names
no model: neither, including a made-up name ('Luna', 'Sam', 'TechCraft AI team'). OWN is decided by
a table in plot.py (LINEAGE), not by the coder: the releasing org, the model's name, or its base
model's org or name, so Tulu naming Meta is own and zephyr naming Mistral is own. The table covers
every model drawn (asserted).
