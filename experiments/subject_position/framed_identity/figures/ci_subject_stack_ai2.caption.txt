PLATE: WHAT THE SPEAKER SAYS IT IS, WITH AI SPLIT INTO SELF AND OTHER. "Who are you?", one coder,
three model sets.

Each bar is one condition's answers split by the kind of thing the speaker claims to be, and the AI
answers further by the name they give. Value: per model, the share of its answers in that group; the
bar prints the MEAN over models (each model once; each bar sums to 100, asserted). Labels inside
segments are rounded to whole percents and printed only where the segment is at least 5 points wide;
exact values are below. The two AI slices sum to the AI segment of ci_subject_stack4 (asserted).

AI (self): an AI answer whose self_name or maker_named matches the speaker's OWN lineage. AI
(other): every other AI answer -- it names no model at all ('I am an AI assistant'), gives a made-up
name ('Luna', 'Sam', 'TechCraft AI team'), or names a model or lab not its own (OpenAI, Anthropic;
that wins where an answer names both). The three-way split behind AI (other) is in
ci_subject_stack_ai and below. OWN is decided by a table in plot.py (LINEAGE), not by the coder: the
releasing org, the model's name, or its base model's org or name, so Tulu naming Meta is own and
zephyr naming Mistral is own. The table covers every model drawn (asserted).

Other groups as ci_subject_stack4: a person; something else (named character, or thing or idea);
no identity claim.
