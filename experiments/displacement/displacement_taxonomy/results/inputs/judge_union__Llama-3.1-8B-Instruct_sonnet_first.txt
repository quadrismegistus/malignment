Two people were shown the same table below and asked what relation connects the
words on each side. Neither was given any vocabulary; each invented its own names.
Their two readings follow. You are not told which is which, and they were produced
under different conditions.

Judge the readings against the table. You are not being asked which you prefer in
style, nor which is longer. Judge whether each relation is SUPPORTED by the words
and positions actually shown, whether any relation is OVER-READ (stated more
strongly than the movement justifies, or resting on words that barely moved), and
whether either reading MISSES something the table plainly shows.

Note: a relation names a group on each side and what connects them. A relation
with nothing on one side is a list, not a relation; say so if you see one.
