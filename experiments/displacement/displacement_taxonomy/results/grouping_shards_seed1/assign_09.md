     One group consists of verbs that denote physical actions or events (signing, releasing, proceeding), while the other group consists of verbs that denote communicative or causative acts (telling, saying, giving, writing, wanting, letting).
[1554] Decided to do something to or with him vs. decided to feel or be something toward him
     One group names actions that are done to, with, or in relation to the other person (take, follow, leave, let, keep, give, stay, put, trust), while the other group names inner states or life events involving the other person (love, believe, kill, obey, marry, tell, die). The relation is between outward, manipulable acts and inward or fateful states/events.
