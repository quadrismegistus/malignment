     One group consists of verbs that describe an action the subject initiates or performs, often with an object or direction; the other group consists of verbs that describe a state or event that follows, often without an object or with the subject as experiencer. The relation is between initiating an action and undergoing or continuing a state.
[1364] Continuation of the same action versus a new action
     One group continues or elaborates the act of opening the letter, while the other group introduces a subsequent, separate action or reaction.
[1449] manner of motion vs. change of state
     One group names a process or manner of motion that can continue over time, while the other names a telic change of state or endpoint.
[894] Motion vs. Violation
     One group consists of verbs of motion or travel, while the other consists of verbs of destruction or desecration.
