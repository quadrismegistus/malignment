[1026] Temporal immediacy vs. degree/contrast
     One side specifies the exact moment or immediacy of the action, while the other side expresses degree or a contrasting temporal frame.
[1722] Continuation type after a conditional clause
     One group continues the sentence by naming the speech act that introduced the condition, while the other continues with a connective or existential that adds a further qualification or clause without naming an act.
