## The axes

### `inner_state_vs_outward_act` — inner state vs. outward act

**pole_x** mental, perceptual, or affective event inside the subject · **pole_y** outwardly performed, observable act

One side names something thought, felt, perceived, realized, wanted or held as a stance; the other names an act carried out in the world, whether physical or spoken.

## What the consolidator said it could not do

Scale check: the four axes every reader proposed (speech/physical, inner/outward, same-event/new-event, content/framing) plus act-vs-state, manner, argument structure, target and orientation carry the bulk of the 2,466 readings; the tail axes below ~n=10 per reader are kept only where two or more readers independently reached for them, or where the distinction has no home elsewhere.

Deliberate splits that could be collapsed further if you want fewer axes: (a) `target_person_vs_thing` vs `orientation_toward_other_party` — one is what kind of object the act takes, the other whether a second party is engaged at all; four readers proposed both, which is why I kept them apart, but they will compete for the same readings. (b) `act_vs_state` vs `act_vs_outcome` — aspectual (dynamic/stative) against causal (act/its consequence); four readers proposed both, so the split is evidenced, but readers who proposed only one will have pooled them. (c) `argument_structure` vs `word_class_or_form` — complement demands against part-of-speech and inflection; several reader proposals (s10 "word class or syntactic frame", s15 "Syntactic frame of the filler") straddle both and I assigned by their stated emphasis. (d) `deliberation_vs_decisive_act` overlaps `inner_state_vs_outward_act` on stance readings; I kept it because its interrogative side ("asking how to proceed") is a speech act, not an inner state, and twelve readers named it separately.

Loose fits I made rather than leave orphans: s9 "immediacy of the next event" went into `force_and_abruptness` on the abrupt/gradual pole, though it is really temporal; s4 "manner vs. path" went into `manner_vs_act` even though both its poles are specifications; s14 "aptness to the activity" went into `specificity_vs_generality` as domain-fit.

Could not merge cleanly (2 proposals, left out): s1 "additive vs restrictive continuation" (both poles are connectives, so it sits under no pole pair I have — a discourse-relation-type axis would be needed, and one reader is thin evidence for it); s11 "bodily mode" (release/emission vs seizure/rigidity — a real contrast but both poles are involuntary bodily events, fitting neither force nor volition).

Weakest axis in the set: `co_member_of_same_field`. Its poles are not nameable in the way the instruction asks — it is the residual category for contrasts that are lexical-field swaps with no general dimension. Keep it as a catch-all or drop it and let those readings fall to `specificity_vs_generality`.

Possibly missing: nothing in the sixteen proposals distinguishes readings by the *frame* being completed (narrative continuation vs. dialogue vs. list), which several readers gesture at from different directions (s15 "managing the discourse", s3 "grammatical form of the filler", s10 "word class or syntactic frame"); if the corpus has that structure it is currently spread across three axes. Also absent: any axis about animacy of the *subject* rather than the object, and any axis about tense/temporal location of the whole clause as opposed to a modifier within it.

**`speech_vs_physical_act` REVERSES ACROSS THE RANGE, AND THE MARGINAL TEST AVERAGED THE REVERSAL AWAY.** Bottom tertile: 44 frames where the SPEAKING side falls against 29 where the physical side does (p=0.1). Middle: 42 against 37 (p=0.65), flat. Top: **7 against 81** (p=4.5e-17). So *the deed becomes an utterance* is a high-lift phenomenon and nothing else — at low lift the axis leans the other way. That is why the best-attested axis in the run (16 of 16 readers) carries one of its weakest corpus-wide q values, 0.0023: a marginal binomial over a sign that flips with dose reports the residue of a cancellation.

`sexual_or_transgressive_content` does not reverse; it does not EXIST below the top tertile (0 and 1 placed relations against 19). `force_and_abruptness` both emerges and sharpens: 4/2, then 9/0, then 39/1.
