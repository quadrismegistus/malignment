## Against the ten meta-relations

Four of the eighteen are the folder's existing vocabulary arriving by a different route: **the deed becomes an utterance** is *blow becomes voice*; **the grievance is routed through a procedure** is *proceduralization*; **the charged referent goes ordinary** is *charged referent drained to a benign one*; and `co_member_of_same_field`'s 41 abstentions are *same-field reshuffle*, which shows up here as a refusal to place rather than as a direction.

**The names are mine and the directions are not.** Every movement above was measured by a reader who saw two unlabelled word lists in an order drawn per relation, against poles named by readers who saw no words at all.
