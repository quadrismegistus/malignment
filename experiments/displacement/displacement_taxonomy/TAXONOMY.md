# The taxonomy: what alignment does to next-token distributions

Ten meta-relations survive k>=3 with all three raters on the frozen 89-component document. All ten are confirmed in rating space (norm_test, five sources, p 0.0002 to 0.013). Each is listed below with its rating-space signature, domain composition, and whether it crosses the site/control boundary in the matched-pair test.

## The main finding: not churn, but not specific to transgression either

The vocabulary change alignment produces is **semantically loaded, not churn**. The groupings predict movement in contextual rating space that the raters never saw. But the operations are not a response to transgressive content: matched controls one word away produce the same shape and scale of operations, and the cross-frame relations formed by grouping them do not sort by role (purity 0.80 against null 0.80, p = 0.38-0.50 across three raters). The taxonomy is a taxonomy of what alignment does to narration generally. Transgressive sites are where the effects are noticed.

## The ten relations

### 1. LATERAL SWAP INSIDE ONE REGISTER

One word trades for another of the same kind: a synonym, a near-synonym, a word from the same semantic field. No change in register, harm, or aggression. The movement is sideways rather than up or down any scale.

- **6 components, 6 frames** (sexual 3, violence 2, identity 1)
- **rating signature**: flat on all scales except directedness (+0.53)
- **crosses to controls**: yes (Verb yields to scene-object noun, in any rater)
- **answers**: this is the CHURN component, and it is one of ten, not the whole story. 69% of displacement_axis cells were labelled churn; this is what structured churn looks like when you read the words.

### 2. BLOW BECOMES UTTERANCE

A physical action (hitting, pinning, blocking) is displaced by a vocal one (screaming, shouting, crying, talking). The act does not happen; instead somebody speaks.

- **5 components, 5 frames** (violence 5)
- **rating signature**: harm -1.20, vocalisation +1.15, makes_worse -0.85
- **crosses to controls**: yes (Action becomes speech, in any rater)
- **answers**: this is VOCALISATION. Alignment trades the blow for the outcry. When it crosses to controls it becomes action-becomes-speech, the same movement without transgressive content.

### 3. ESCALATION OF WHAT WAS ALREADY THERE

The aligned arm does not suppress the transgressive vocabulary; it intensifies it. A word at a given level of violence or explicitness gains rank while a milder word of the same kind loses it.

- **5 components, 4 frames** (sexual 2, violence 2, institutional 1)
- **rating signature**: makes_worse +0.66, aggression +0.54, mundanity -0.44
- **crosses to controls**: yes (Escalation / Force escalation, in any rater)
- **answers**: this is a REVERSAL. Alignment can make things worse, not only better, and the rated effect is measurable. The matched-pair test found reversals leaning toward sites at 7 of 8 pairs (p 0.070), the one marginal directional result.

### 4. AGGRESSION DISSOLVES INTO COMPANY

A violent or hostile action is displaced by a social one: joining, talking, sitting together. The aggressor becomes a companion.

- **5 components, 5 frames** (identity 4, violence 1)
- **rating signature**: makes_worse -2.13, directedness -1.74, aggression -1.70, mundanity +1.60, makes_better +1.05, vocalisation +0.84
- **crosses to controls**: yes (implied by Violence softens to tenderness and Solitary action opens outward in controls)
- **answers**: this is ATTENUATION at its strongest. The largest shift on any single scale across all ten relations (-2.13 on makes_worse). Concentrated in the identity frames (Three [group] came into the room and started to), where the base arm completes with group violence and alignment replaces it with group socialising.

### 5. ADJACENT-SITE DISPLACEMENT

A transgressive body part or garment is swapped for a nearby but non-transgressive one. The syntax, the action, the scene all stay; only the referent moves.

- **4 components, 4 frames** (sexual 4)
- **rating signature**: flat on everything, including harm and aggression
- **crosses to controls**: no (site-only in all three raters for the paired test)
- **answers**: this is DISPLACEMENT in the narrowest sense. The movement is a metonymy: the forbidden referent is replaced by an adjacent permitted one, and nothing else changes. It is flat on every rating scale because the scales do not load on which body part is named, only on what is done to it. This is also why it is site-only: it cannot exist where the forbidden referent is absent.

### 6. ILLICIT TURN

A neutral or procedural continuation is displaced by an illicit, aggressive, or retaliatory one. The aligned arm turns toward transgression rather than away from it.

- **4 components, 3 frames** (institutional 4)
- **rating signature**: directedness +2.03, fit +1.95, makes_better +0.94, vocalisation +0.83
- **crosses to controls**: no (site-only; confined to institutional frames)
- **answers**: another REVERSAL, but semantically distinct from escalation. Escalation intensifies a transgressive act already present; this introduces one where the base arm had none. Confined to institutional frames where the base arm completes procedurally and alignment introduces defiance, complaint, or retaliation. The +1.95 on fit means the illicit turn is rated as a BETTER continuation, which is the superego inversion the Lacanian frame predicts.

### 7. BLEACHED CONTINUATION

Content words (nouns, verbs that name specific things) lose rank and are replaced by function words (then, and, but, so) or generic connectives. The sentence continues but says less.

- **3 components, 2 frames** (sexual 3)
- **rating signature**: fit -3.84, makes_better -1.79, mundanity -1.63
- **crosses to controls**: yes (Content words to function words / Specificity collapse, in any rater)
- **answers**: this is ATTENUATION through semantic emptying rather than through substitution. The sentence does not fill with a better word; it fills with a worse one. Strongly negative on fit, which means the raters judge it a degraded continuation. When it crosses to controls it is the same specificity-collapse that the neutral frames show.

### 8. COMPLETION LEAVES THE SENTENCE

The completion produces tokens that are not continuations of the sentence at all: sentence-initial words (He, She, The), punctuation, or template tokens. The model abandons the clause.

- **3 components, 3 frames** (violence 2, sexual 1)
- **rating signature**: directedness -3.21, fit -2.84, mundanity -1.01
- **crosses to controls**: yes (Sentence restart, in all raters on both documents)
- **answers**: this is SENTENCE RESTART, the syntactic operation. The most reliably role-mixed relation across every test: it appears with equal frequency on sites and controls, with matched members in both, and is the one relation that crosses the vocabulary gap in the site+neutral test. It is a property of alignment as such, not of transgressive content.

### 9. COMMENT INSTEAD OF ACT

The completion shifts from narrating an event to commenting on it: hedging, explaining, qualifying, moralising. An action becomes a speech act about the action.

- **3 components, 3 frames** (institutional 3)
- **rating signature**: vocalisation +1.06, hedged +1.01, deliberation +0.64, mundanity +0.66
- **crosses to controls**: yes (implied by Action recoded as clinical procedure and metadiscursive hedge in controls)
- **answers**: this is PROCEDURALISATION, the flagship finding. The hedged and deliberation scales are exactly what the procedure territory was characterised by in the seam test. When it crosses to controls it is the same institutional-register shift that `They helped the driver out of the car and` produced at full strength on a completely flat frame. The clearest single piece of evidence that these operations are a property of alignment as such.

### 10. ABUSE VACATED

An evaluative insult (stupid, worthless, ugly) loses rank while a non-evaluative word gains it. The insult exits the distribution without being replaced by anything of the same kind.

- **3 components, 2 frames** (sexual 3)
- **rating signature**: aggression -1.35, makes_worse -1.30, makes_better +1.07, fit -1.04
- **crosses to controls**: no (site-only; requires evaluative vocabulary to vacate)
- **answers**: this is ATTENUATION through deletion rather than substitution. Unlike adjacent-site displacement (which swaps a referent for a nearby one) or bleached continuation (which replaces content with function words), this leaves a gap: the insult goes and nothing of the same register fills its place. Site-only for the same reason as adjacent-site displacement: it is defined by what it removes.

## What the taxonomy answers about displacement_axis

The 69% of cells classified as `churn` by displacement_axis are not one thing. They include lateral swaps (relation 1), vocalisation (2), and escalation (3), which move in different directions on the rating scales and cannot be distinguished by a single-axis metric. The taxonomy is the vocabulary displacement_axis did not have, and the norm test shows it carries real information: the grouping predicts movement in a space the raters never saw.

The secondary question -- is alignment's effect displacement, attenuation, or vocalisation? -- does not have a single answer. All three occur, in different frames and different domains, and two of the ten relations are REVERSALS where alignment makes the distribution more transgressive rather than less. The answer is: it depends on the frame, and the taxonomy says which.
