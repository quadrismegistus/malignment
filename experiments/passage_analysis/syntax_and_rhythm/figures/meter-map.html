Metrical uncertainty and tension, human and LLM

Every point is a mean over 10-syllable windows cut at word boundaries, with lineation ignored, and scanned with the five constraints of the 2020 antimetricality study (s ≤ 2, w ≤ 2). Uncertainty is the number of viable scansions per window. Tension (MTS) is the sum of constraint violations across those scansions. Lower is more metrical. The first figure compares human and model prose and verse like for like. The second places every group measured so far against all the human baselines.

Prose and verse: people and models

Seven lines. The human prose curves are fiction and non-fiction from the antimetricality reparse, dated by publication year. Human verse uses only the poets' own continuations of the poems the models were asked to continue, dated by the year the author turned 30. Model verse uses only those same continuations, placed at the period of the poem being continued. Model prose has no period, so it is drawn as a level. Right-hand values are overall means.

Hover for values, ±1.96 standard errors across poems, passages or authors, and sample sizes. Hollow points have fewer than 25 authors or 50 poems. Model verse is Llama-3.1-8B and Mistral-7B pooled, base (text) against instruct, continuing the same poems.

AI verse sits at the Augustan minimum

Base and aligned models continuing the same human poems land at tension ≈ 4.7. That is as regular as the most metrical human period, 1700–1749 (4.44), and well below the poets' own continuations (6.46). The Olmo-3 pilot is an exception: it sits near 19th-century levels.

Base prose is more metrical than any human fiction

Base-model prose (9.42) sits below every period of human fiction (10.1–11.6) on both axes. Aligned prose (11.01) moves inside the human range, toward non-fiction.

Alignment widens the verse/prose gap

Prose minus verse tension is 4.7 for base models and 6.3 for aligned ones. The human gap peaked at 6.4 in 1700–1749 and fell to 0.8 by 1950–1999.

Read with care

  • Verse and prose come from different prompts and generation setups. The verse continuations came from 4-bit ollama models; the prose came from full-precision HF generation with 3,000-token caps.
  • Only the prose results are a registered test (syntax_and_rhythm, H2). There, after controlling for monosyllables, aligned prose is not significantly more tense (13/34 lineages in the predicted direction), but it is more often imperfect on *s/unstressed (31/34, p = 7.7e-7). Everything verse-side is exploratory.
  • Periods run 1600–1999; 2000–2049 is left out everywhere, since it is not a full half-century and is sparse. The human prose curves are thin at the early end: Fiction 1600–1649 has 20 authors and 1700–1749 has 13.
  • The Olmo-3 pilot is 12 primers × 2 samples per rung. Its own docstring says it is never to be quoted as a result.
  • 0.14% of prose windows were skipped by the pronunciation-pooling guard. Windows parsed before the guard existed have not had the same rule applied retroactively.
