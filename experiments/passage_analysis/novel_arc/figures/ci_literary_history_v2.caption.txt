CAVEATS
- The concreteness and interiority years are Chicago years. Where the two corpora overlap, in the
1880s, Chicago sits 0.06 z more abstract than Chadwyck (21 Chadwyck texts, 113 Chicago).
- The interiority history rests on the lexicon alone; the arm effect was also established, and more
strongly, by an LLM coder reading passages blind for degree of interiority
(interiority_in_passages).
- The rhythm arms set prose from 34 lineages against verse from two model families; early decades
hold few fiction texts (see the counts).
- One rhythm decade is not drawn: the 1700s, at 2.96, from 4 fiction texts, lies above the panel's
window. It is in the data and in the smooth; the axis only stops short of it.
- API models are not drawn: there is no API prose for the rhythm panel, and RH removed the arm from
all three.
