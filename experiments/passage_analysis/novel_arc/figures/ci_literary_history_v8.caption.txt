REWINDING LITERARY HISTORY (version 8). Two measures of English fiction, 1600-2000, with the
base and aligned model arms as horizontal lines.

Lines: base models dotted, aligned models dashed, labelled at the right. Gray points: decade values
of the human history; black line: their lowess smooth (span 0.3). Open circle: where an arm's line
crosses the smooth, with a short leader to the year. No API arm is drawn (RH).

CONCRETENESS (rh_absconc_median, z; up = more concrete). Per text the median over its passages; per
decade the median over texts; Chadwyck 1600-1879 (1,333 texts), Chicago from 1880 (9,089 texts);
decades under 3 texts dropped. Arms, as in the interiority panel: see ARMS below.
  arms: Base models +0.0807, Aligned models -0.0613; paired, aligned minus base: more abstract in 23 of 25, median -0.123.
  crossings: Base models 1965; Aligned models 1914
  texts per decade: 1600:4, 1620:8, 1630:6, 1640:7, 1650:16, 1660:14, 1670:11, 1680:23, 1690:15, 1700:3, 1710:6, 1720:26, 1730:8, 1740:13, 1750:17, 1760:15, 1770:7, 1780:6, 1790:49, 1800:19, 1810:29, 1820:91, 1830:208, 1840:272, 1850:176, 1860:135, 1870:96, 1880:113, 1890:190, 1900:372, 1910:423, 1920:472, 1930:656, 1940:679, 1950:487, 1960:493, 1970:834, 1980:1554, 1990:2465, 2000:351

INTERIORITY (usas_x: the percentage of a passage's content words tagged in USAS field X,
psychological actions, states and processes; measure_lltk.py), same recipe.
  arms: Base models 14.29%, Aligned models 16.34%; paired, aligned minus base: up in 24 of 25, median +2.2 points.
  crossings: none; both arms sit above the whole history (max 13.71%).

ARMS (panels 1 and 2): each model's median over its own passages, then the median over the 25
matched pairs' models -- lineages (roster.lineages()) holding both a base and an aligned model in
model_placement.parquet, one of each per lineage; 26 base and 28 aligned models before pairing, so
bloom-7b1, Falcon3-10B-Instruct, Falcon3-1B-Instruct, Falcon3-3B-Instruct drop out unpartnered. 2140
base and 2490 aligned passages within the pairs. No floor on passages per model. The v6 plate took
the median over pooled passages, which lets a model with many passages outweigh one with few; this
counts each model once.

CAVEATS
- The concreteness and interiority years are Chicago years. Where the two corpora overlap, in the
1880s, Chicago sits 0.06 z more abstract than Chadwyck (21 Chadwyck texts, 113 Chicago).
- The interiority history rests on the lexicon alone; the arm effect was also established, and more
strongly, by an LLM coder reading passages blind for degree of interiority
(interiority_in_passages).
- API models are not drawn (RH).
- The verse/prose rhythm panel of earlier versions is left out (RH).
