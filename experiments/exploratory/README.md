**`misc/` is gone, deliberately.** A folder named for having no organising idea
accumulates whatever nobody placed, and nothing in it is findable by what it is
about. `exploratory` at least names a STATUS -- not yet promoted -- which is a
fact about the work rather than an absence of one.

**And the departure is the point of the status.** `subject_position` was
exploratory for eleven days and is now a subject with two open questions it did
not have when it arrived. That is what this folder is for: a place where work can
be held without a claim until it earns one, rather than a place where it settles.
